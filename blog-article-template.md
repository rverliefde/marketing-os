## Writing reminders

- Sentence case for all headings — never title case
- No full stops at the end of headings
- Only one H1 per article
- H3 must always be nested under H2
- British English spelling throughout
- No Oxford comma
- Contractions always: "we're" not "we are"
- Max 20 words per sentence
- Max 5 sentences per paragraph
- Active voice — never passive
- No exclamation marks
- Benefits before features
- Always end with a clear CTA
