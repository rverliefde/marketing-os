# Example: Industry/Vertical Positioning Post
**Source:** blog.clicksend.com/sms-health-tech/ — "SMS for Health Tech: Why you need a robust API, not just basic SMS"
**Author:** Anna Christensen | **Category:** Business Solutions | **Length:** ~3 min read

## Content type
Industry-specific positioning piece aimed at a B2B platform buyer (health tech companies), not an end consumer. Makes the case for "why basic SMS isn't enough for your industry."

## Structure pattern
1. **Provocative opening question** — challenges the reader's current assumption about their provider
2. **Stakes framing** — establishes why this industry specifically has higher requirements than others
3. **Day-to-day use case table** — two-column format: use case name + one-line description, covering the range of ways the audience already uses the product
4. **Pain point list** — bolded pain point + explanation, framed around what goes wrong with inferior solutions
5. **"Why this matters more here" section** — makes the emotional/business case using a concrete cost statistic
6. **Mid-article product tie-in** — soft pivot to a specific product feature relevant to the pain (not the main product)
7. **Checklist section** — checkmark-style bullet list of evaluation criteria, framed as "what to look for"
8. **Direct comparison table** — mirrors the checklist criteria one-for-one, showing how the company measures up against each
9. **Feature deep-dive** — one differentiating feature explained in more depth near the end
10. **Closing CTA** — question framing + single action link

## Tone
Authoritative, consultative — written as if advising a buyer through an evaluation, not selling directly. Uses industry-specific stats to build credibility.

## Formatting devices
- Two-column tables for use cases and comparisons
- Checkmark bullets (✔) for evaluation criteria
- Bolded lead-in phrases within paragraphs
- Statistics used sparingly but prominently (one hard number per major claim)

## Useful for tool tailoring
Reuse for: vertical/industry landing content, competitive positioning pieces, "why choose us for X industry" content, buyer evaluation guides.
