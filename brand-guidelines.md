# ClickSend brand guidelines

> The Big Blue Brand Book — shortened reference version

---

## Logo

### Full logo
Our logo is the primary identifier for our brand. It captures our name, mission and legacy.

Always ensure ample contrast in colour pairings. The following versions are approved:

| Version | When to use |
|---------|-------------|
| Primary (full colour) | Default — use on light backgrounds |
| Inverted | On dark backgrounds where the primary logo lacks contrast |
| Mono-white | Sparingly on coloured or image backgrounds |
| Mono-black | Last resort — only on light backgrounds when other versions don't work |

### Minimum size
The smallest size for the full logo is **32px height**. Below that, use the logomark only.

### Clear space
Keep the area around the logo clear. Minimum clear space = the logo's x-height. No other elements should interfere with this space.

### Social icon
White logomark on a solid ClickSend Blue (#2977FF) background. Use for social profiles and integration listings.

### Logo don'ts
- Don't stretch or squeeze
- Don't outline
- Don't use unapproved colours
- Don't apply shadows or effects
- Don't rotate
- Don't apply patterns
- Don't place on similarly-coloured or busy backgrounds
- Don't change orientation or proportions
- Don't recreate any elements

## Typography

### Primary typeface — Power Grotesk
Used for headlines and titles. Geometric sans-serif.
- Weights: Ultra Bold, Medium, Regular

### Supporting typeface — Montserrat
Used for body copy and smaller sub-headlines. Geometric sans-serif with high legibility at small sizes.
- Weights: Ultra Bold, Medium

### Legacy web fonts (brand.clicksend.com)

| Font | Usage |
|------|-------|
| Montserrat | Headers and subheaders |
| Open Sans | Body copy, lists, all other copy |
| Vollkorn | Quotes, blog posts, case studies, About us — pages with a personal or storytelling character |
