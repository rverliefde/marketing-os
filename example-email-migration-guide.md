# Example: Transition/Migration Guide (Sensitive Announcement)
**Source:** blog.clicksend.com/email-migration-guide/ — "Making the move: a simple guide to migrating email"
**Author:** Anna Christensen | **Category:** SMS guides | **Length:** ~3 min read

## Content type
A genuinely tricky format: announcing a product shutdown while keeping customer trust, then guiding them through the transition — and using it as an opportunity to cross-sell the company's core product (SMS) without feeling opportunistic.

## Structure pattern
1. **Soft opening** — reframes a negative (product closing) as a positive transition ("find your email a new home"), immediately reassuring ("don't stress, we're here to support you")
2. **Carve-out note** — asterisked clarification for a sub-segment of users who are NOT affected, addressed immediately to reduce panic/confusion
3. **Numbered action steps** — the bulk of the post, each step a clear header + bulleted specifics of what to do
4. **Third-party recommendation** — recommends a specific partner/alternative by name, with reasons, presented as genuine advice rather than an ad
5. **Deadline callout** — clear, non-buried statement of the hard cutoff date, framed constructively ("don't leave this until the last minute")
6. **Pivot section** — "thinking beyond email?" reframes the transition as a chance to reconsider using the company's core product (SMS) instead, using comparison stats
7. **Support close** — reassurance that help is available, both from the third-party partner and internal support, ending on a low-pressure note

## Tone
Empathetic, reassuring, practical. Written to minimize customer anxiety about a negative change. Avoids corporate deflection — direct about what's happening and why.

## Formatting devices
- Numbered major steps, each with sub-bullets
- Bolded step titles
- An embedded comparison stat block (SMS vs email open rates) to support the cross-sell without hard-selling
- Callout/highlight styling implied around the recommended partner section

## Useful for tool tailoring
Reuse for: any deprecation/sunset announcement, migration guides, or "bad news wrapped in helpfulness" content. Also a good template for soft cross-sell — introducing a related product as a genuine suggestion rather than a pitch.
