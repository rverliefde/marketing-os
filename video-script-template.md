## Script format

Use a two-column format: **Visual** (what's on screen) and **VO** (voiceover / what's said).

| Visual | VO (Voiceover) |
|--------|----------------|
| [Describe what appears on screen] | [Write what is spoken, word for word] |
| [Scene / animation / product shot] | [VO line] |
| [Scene] | [VO line] |
| [Scene] | [VO line] |
| [End card / CTA screen] | [VO line + CTA] |

---

## Writing tips for video scripts

**Write like you talk**
Read every line aloud. If it sounds stiff or formal, rewrite it. Contractions are essential — "we're", "you'll", "it's".

**Short sentences only**
One idea per line. If you need to pause for breath, break it into two lines.

**Hook fast**
The first 3–5 seconds decide whether someone keeps watching. Open with a question, a bold statement or an unexpected idea.

**Active voice**
"ClickSend sends your messages" not "Your messages are sent by ClickSend."

**No exclamation marks**
Write with energy, not punctuation.

**End with a clear CTA**
Tell the viewer exactly what to do: "Try ClickSend free", "Get started today", "See how it works".

---

## Tone reminders

- Bold and a little weird — not corporate
- Conversational and energetic
- Benefits-first — why should the viewer care?
- Keep it tight — every word earns its place
