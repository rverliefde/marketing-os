# Customer service email template

---

## Email structure

### Subject line options

**Option 1 — simple, includes account name:**
```
Re: Your [Account Name].
```
Pretext: `Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet.`

**Option 2 — highlights importance:**
```
Important: [This is happening soon.]
```
Pretext: `Please read. There's important information in this email.`

---

### Email body

**Headline:** Insert headline here.

---

**Intro:**
Hi [Name],

[Opening paragraph. Keep it human, warm and get to the point fast. Max 3 sentences.]

---

**Subhead 1:** What you need to do next: [Action].

*Or if no action required:*
**Subhead 1:** [Name], you don't need to do anything.

[Body copy. Clear instructions. Use numbered steps if there are multiple actions.]

---

**Subhead 2:** Any questions?

If you'd like more information, please take a look at [this]. Or you can reach out to your support team or call 1800 155 228.

Speak soon,
[Name]
[ClickSend Customer Service Team]

**Button:** See more.

---

### Johnson box (optional)

Use the Johnson box to showcase information without disrupting the flow of the copy. Ideal for:
- Listing product benefits
- Breaking up complex information
- Explaining steps

**Johnson box structure:**
```
[Short subhead]

• Add a benefit here.
• Add a benefit here.
• Add a benefit here.
• Add a benefit here.

[Short CTA]
```

> **Note:** The Johnson box sits on the right-hand side of the email in the final design.

---

## Writing reminders

- Lead with empathy — acknowledge before instructing
- Use contractions: "you'll", "we're", "don't"
- Short sentences, one idea each
- Active voice always
- No exclamation marks
- Subheadings make long emails scannable
- Always include a clear next step or CTA
