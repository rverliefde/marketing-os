# Helpdoc Structural Pattern Example: Comparison / Decision-Guide Article

Source: https://help.clicksend.com/en/articles/42309-choosing-the-right-sender-id

This example demonstrates the pattern for an article that walks through several options under one umbrella topic. Each option gets a hidden H3 (so it doesn't clutter the accordion with four near-identical nested entries), while the two true navigable topics — "what's available" and "regional examples" — are H2s. A callout is used once, for a single load-bearing warning line, and FAQs close the article.

---

[H1] Choosing the right Sender ID
[ENTER]
A Sender ID is what your recipients see as the number or "sender" when they receive your message — it's an easy way to ensure customers can quickly identify who sent the message.
[ENTER]
Choose how your sender appears by buying or setting up different Sender IDs.
[ENTER]
[DIVIDER]
[H2] What Sender IDs are available?
[ENTER]
ClickSend offers several types of Sender ID. Each has its own advantages and is recommended for different purposes. When purchasing a Sender ID with ClickSend, registration and fees may apply.
[ENTER]
Not all Sender IDs can be used in every country. Please check our guidelines for the country you plan on sending to.
[ENTER]
[H3] Shared numbers
[ENTER]
This is a number that's assigned by ClickSend when you send messages.
[ENTER]
How it works:
[ENTER]
- If you don't set up a Sender ID, you'll send via a shared number by default.
- Shared numbers are shared, so you'll send via a different number each time.
- Replies appear in the ClickSend dashboard, not on your personal device.
- There's no set up required.
- Using Smart Sender automatically assigns a shared number if no custom Sender ID is set, although availability might differ by region.
[ENTER]
When to use shared numbers: shared numbers are ideal for basic messaging when custom Sender IDs aren't required.
[ENTER]
[H3] Dedicated numbers
[ENTER]
These are unique phone numbers purchased through ClickSend. They are specific and will vary depending on what is available in the country.
[ENTER]
You can purchase dedicated numbers through the ClickSend dashboard by navigating to Sender IDs and selecting Buy a Number.
[ENTER]
Types of dedicated numbers: Shortcodes (SC), LongCodes (LVN or LC), and US-specific 10DLC or TFN.
[ENTER]
How it works:
[ENTER]
- Dedicated numbers support two-way communication.
- This number is only used by your business.
- There are different SMS limits per send based on the type of dedicated number.
- To fully enable use, dedicated numbers may require registration or carrier approval processes.
[ENTER]
When to use dedicated numbers: for customer support or high-value, personalised interactions, and for campaigns that require guaranteed inbound message delivery.
[ENTER]
[H3] Alpha tags
[ENTER]
This is a custom text-based sender name that can be up to 11 characters with no spaces.
[ENTER]
How it works:
[ENTER]
- Appears as a custom name (e.g. "MyBrand").
- You can't receive replies to any messages sent via Alpha Tag.
- Alpha Tags can't be used for conversational messaging.
[ENTER]
When to use alpha tags: perfect for when you need to brand your messages or any time you're sending one-way communication.
[ENTER]
[H3] Own numbers
[ENTER]
This is when you use your personal or business phone number as the Sender ID.
[ENTER]
How it works:
[ENTER]
- Messages are sent from ClickSend using your number.
- Your replies will go directly to your own number.
[ENTER]
When to use own numbers: when you want replies to appear on your personal phone or internal systems. It's great for small-scale, highly personalised communication.
[ENTER]
Own numbers need to be verified with a code that will be sent to the number you're registering.
[ENTER]
[DIVIDER]
[H2] Regional examples
[ENTER]
Many countries have their own regulations for Sender IDs. They may have rules about the types of sender you can use, and you may also need to register your Sender ID before you use it.
[ENTER]
[H3] United States
[ENTER]
[CALLOUT]
Alpha Tags cannot be used for US messaging, even if you own a fully registered dedicated number or have ported your personal number.
[ENTER]
Messaging requires a fully registered dedicated number, and ported numbers become virtual SMS numbers (not capable of voice calling) and still require proper registration.
[ENTER]
[H3] Australia
[ENTER]
In Australia, you have three main Sender ID options:
[ENTER]
- Shared or Dedicated Virtual Number: displays a phone number as the sender ID.
- Alphanumeric Sender ID: displays a custom name, such as your company brand, but is not reply-capable.
- Own number: displays your own number. Replies will be sent back to your device.

[FAQ]

[EXPANDABLE]
Question: Can I use multiple Sender IDs?
Answer: Yes. You can register and switch between different Sender IDs depending on the campaign or region.

[EXPANDABLE]
Question: What if my region doesn't support my chosen Sender ID?
Answer: ClickSend will guide you to an alternative option, such as a Dedicated Number or default Shared Number, based on the region's regulations. Contact customer support for help.

[EXPANDABLE]
Question: Can I use a dedicated number to receive verification codes from other services?
Answer: All ClickSend numbers are Virtual Numbers — cloud-based numbers not tied to a specific physical SIM card or landline. ClickSend does not support receiving messages from Alpha Tags; this isn't supported and can have security implications.
